Health insurance in Rwanda

Mutuelle de Santé & your insurance options

Your cover can help you access care at participating facilities. Acceptance depends on your scheme, the facility and the service you need. Confirm these details before your visit.

Mutuelle de Santé

Community-based health insurance. Ask the facility about eligibility, referral requirements and any contribution you need to pay.

RSSB medical schemes & private insurance

Check that the facility accepts your plan. Your insurer can confirm benefits, authorisation requirements and any fees not covered.

Paying directly

If you don't have cover, or your service isn't covered, ask for the total cost. Sōlea shows consultation fees in RWF; tests and other services may cost extra.

Care that fits your needs

Online consultations for conversations, advice and follow-up from your phone. Confirm whether your plan covers virtual care.

In-person appointments for examinations, screening and other care at a facility. Ask about cover for any tests or procedures separately.
