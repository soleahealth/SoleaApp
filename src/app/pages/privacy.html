What we collect

Your account details, the answers you give at sign-up (name, age group, district, language, health journeys), the health entries you choose to log, and your appointments.

How we use it

To run your account, show relevant care, and let professionals you book with see the details needed for your visit. We never sell your data.

Who can see it

Your health logs are visible only to you. A professional sees your booking details only for visits you book with them.

Your rights

You can view, correct or delete your information at any time from your profile, or by contacting us.
