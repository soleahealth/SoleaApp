Yet, finding reliable reproductive health information, knowing where to seek care and connecting with the right healthcare professional can still be challenging for many women across Africa.

We're here to help change that.

What we're building

Sōlea is an Africa focused digital sexual and reproductive health platform designed to make healthcare easier to navigate and access.

We're building a connected space where women can explore trusted health information, map out available healthcare services, discover verified clinics and qualified healthcare professionals, and find care that meets their needs.

By bringing healthcare providers, facilities and services into one accessible platform, we aim to make it easier for women to understand their options, find the right support and navigate their reproductive health journeys with confidence.

From knowing where to go to knowing who to turn to, Sōlea helps connect the dots between women and the care they deserve.

Our mission

To make sexual and reproductive healthcare more accessible, understandable and connected for women and girls across Africa through technology, trusted expertise and meaningful partnerships.

What we believe

Access is essential. Quality reproductive healthcare should not feel out of reach.

Knowledge creates confidence. Every woman deserves reliable information to make informed decisions about her health.

Trust comes first. Healthcare must be built on credible expertise, privacy, respect and compassion.

Better care is connected care. Technology can help bridge the gaps between individuals, healthcare professionals and facilities.

Our vision

A future where every woman and girl can confidently map out her reproductive health journey, access trusted care and feel supported at every stage of life.

Sōlea. Your reproductive health, connected.
